Our Story & Mission

Making Campus Living in Kenya Safe, Honest & Affordable

Every semester, thousands of students admitted to Kenyan universities face the same chaotic ritual: wandering through unfamiliar neighborhoods, dealing with unvetted middlemen who demand hefty viewing fees, and ending up in buildings with dry taps or unreliable electricity.

Homely Student Residents was founded to solve this dilemma. We operate physical student housing hubs across Nairobi and Kiambu, inspecting every single bedsitter and one-bedroom apartment before listing it. We verify borehole water supply, private electricity token meters, perimeter security, and lease legitimacy.

  • For Students: Transparent KSh pricing, zero viewing fees, fast Wi-Fi, and walking distance to lecture halls.
  • For Parents: Total peace of mind knowing your child resides in a secure compound with dedicated caretakers and clear deposit protection.
  • For Landlords: Respectful, verified student tenants who respect house rules and community living standards.

Our 4-Point Student Housing Standard

Before any bedsitter or one-bedroom is added to Homely, it must pass rigorous physical checks by our local agents.

Continuous Water Supply

High-capacity overhead storage tanks and active borehole pumps to ensure you never run out of water during exam season.

Private Sub-Meters & Tokens

Pay only for what you consume via your own KPLC meter. No unfair flat electricity bills divided among unknown neighbors.

Perimeter & Nighttime Security

Gated compounds with security guards, biometric entrance locks, and well-lit access pathways to student bus stages.

Deposit Protection & Clear Receipts

Standardized agreements that stipulate refund conditions when completing your studies or graduating.
