1. Physical Unit Verification Protocol

We never publish listings submitted over the phone or internet without physical inspection.

Before any bedsitter or one-bedroom appears on Homely Student Residents, our field agents visit the building in person. They run taps to verify borehole flow and water pressure, test electrical sockets, verify that private KPLC sub-meters are properly sealed, and ensure door locks operate smoothly.

  • Water test: Confirmation of minimum 5,000-liter backup tank capacity.
  • Electricity check: Independent prepaid token meters for each room.
  • Wi-Fi check: Testing router signal strength and speed test within the unit.

2. Transparent Student Pricing (No Viewing Fees)

Traditional rental brokers in Kenyan university areas typically demand KSh 500 to KSh 1,500 upfront merely to show you around. At Homely, viewings are completely free. You only pay for rent and deposit when you have inspected the property and signed the agreement with the property manager.

3. Campus Proximity & Commute Safety

Walking home after late-night laboratory sessions or group study at the university library requires well-lit, secure routes. We audit the walking paths between each listed property and the main campus gates (e.g. JKUAT Gate A/C, USIU Gate, KU Footbridge) to ensure students can travel safely.

4. Deposit Protection Guidelines

Every landlord listing on Homely commits to standard rental guidelines: a 1-month deposit policy, clear deduction rules strictly limited to verifiable damage, and defined refund timelines when moving out after semester end.
