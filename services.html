Designed exclusively for university students in Kenya. Safe neighborhoods, budget-friendly options, verified deposits, and on-ground viewing support.

Verified Listings

Every bedsitter and one-bedroom is physically visited and checked by our student agents before being posted. No fake photos or misleading descriptions.

Budget-Friendly Options

Honest rental rates in Kenya Shillings ranging from KSh 7,500 to KSh 22,000/month. Filter easily to find rooms matching your semester budget.

Near Campuses

Units located within a 5-10 minute walk or short direct matatu ride to KU, USIU, JKUAT, MMU, and Daystar so you save on daily commuting fares.

Safe Neighborhoods

Compounds equipped with perimeter walls, electric fences, CCTV, and trusted 24-hour caretakers, giving both students and parents complete security.

Flexible Deposits

Official receipts, standard 1-month deposit policies, and clear agreements outlining refund procedures upon completion of your academic year.

Viewing Support

Dedicated student agents who meet you near your university gate to guide you through multiple units in person with zero viewing fees.
